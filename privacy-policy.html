Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 7, 2026

Daily Thesis is built to keep your reading experience local and minimal. This page explains what the app stores on your device, what it sends over the network, and what third-party services it relies on — including the ads that keep the app free.

What Daily Thesis stores on your device

The app stores a small amount of information locally so it can personalize recommendations and remember your preferences. None of this leaves your device except where noted below.

  • Your name, if you enter it during onboarding or in Settings.
  • Your selected subjects and matched interest categories.
  • Your theme preference (System, Light, or Dark).
  • Your paper history, so you can revisit papers you've previously been shown.

What Daily Thesis sends over the network

The app requests a daily paper recommendation from its backend service, optionally including your selected subjects so the recommendation is relevant to you. No account or persistent user identifier is attached to this request.

When you tap "Read Full Publication," "Cite," or "Share," the app opens external links or system share sheets using your device's own apps and services.

Advertising

Daily Thesis is free to use and supported by ads served through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as your device's advertising ID), general location derived from IP address, and app usage data. This is handled by Google, not by Daily Thesis directly — see Google's Privacy Policy for details on how that data is used.

You can review or reset your device's ad personalization settings at any time in your phone's system settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

Discuss with ChatGPT

If you tap "Discuss with ChatGPT" on a paper, the app opens ChatGPT with that paper's title, authors, and abstract pre-filled into a prompt for you to review and send. This is publicly available information about the paper itself, not personal data about you. The app does not send anything to OpenAI unless you tap this button, and the request happens directly between your device and ChatGPT — Daily Thesis is not in the middle of it. Use of ChatGPT is subject to OpenAI's Privacy Policy.

What Daily Thesis does not do

  • It does not require an account or login.
  • It does not sell your personal information.
  • It does not store your reading history, name, or preferences on a company server — they stay on your device.

Children's privacy

Daily Thesis is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us using the details below and we'll remove it.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect product or legal changes. The current version will always be published at this URL, with the "Last updated" date above reflecting the most recent revision.
