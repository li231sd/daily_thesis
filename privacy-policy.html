Privacy Policy

Daily Thesis is designed to keep your reading experience local, minimal, and private. This page explains what data the app stores, what it sends over the network, and what it does not do.

What Daily Thesis stores

The app stores a small amount of information locally on your device so it can personalize recommendations and remember your preferences.

  • Your name, if you enter it during onboarding or in Settings.
  • Your selected interests and matched subject categories.
  • Your theme preference, including System, Light, or Dark mode.
  • Your paper history, so you can revisit previously recommended papers.

What the app sends

Daily Thesis makes network requests to fetch paper recommendations from its paper service. If you choose interests, the app may include a subject parameter so it can return a more relevant paper.

The app may also open external links when you tap a paper, cite, or share a recommendation. Those actions use Apple system services or third-party apps you choose.

What Daily Thesis does not do

  • It does not require an account.
  • It does not sell your personal information.
  • It does not use your data for advertising.
  • It does not store your reading history on a company server.

Third-party services

Daily Thesis relies on external paper sources and standard platform services for opening links, sharing content, and storing local preferences. Those services may have their own privacy policies and terms.

Children’s privacy

Daily Thesis is not directed to children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated occasionally to reflect product or legal changes. The latest version will always be published at this URL.

Contact
