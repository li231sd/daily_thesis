Math, rendered properly

CS and physics abstracts are full of LaTeX. Daily Thesis renders equations inline instead of leaving you to decode raw markup.

Read without a connection

Papers are prefetched and cached automatically, so your queue is still there on a plane, in a lecture hall, or anywhere else with spotty signal.

Push notifications

Get pinged the moment a new paper matching your interests lands, instead of checking back and hoping.

Swipe through your feed

Skip what's not relevant and dig into what is with a quick swipe, no scrolling through walls of abstracts.

Save what matters

Like a paper and it's saved for later, so you can build a running list without losing track in a sea of tabs.

Feedback that improves your feed

Tell it what's useful and what's not. Your recommendations get sharper the more you read.

1

Join the group
